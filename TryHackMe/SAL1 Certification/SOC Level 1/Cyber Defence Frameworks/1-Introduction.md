# Introduction to Security Analyst Tier 1
* Triage Specialist

### Responsibilities
* Monitor/investigate alerts.
* Configure/manage security tools.
* Develop/implement basic IDS signatures.
* Participate in SOC groups/meetings.
* Create tickets and escalate security incident to Tier 2/SOC Manager if necessary.

### Most Common Skills
* Networking (OSI Model or TCP/IP Model).
* Operating Systems.
* Web Applications.
* Scripting/Programming is a plus.

## Security Operations Centre (SOC)
Its core function is to investigate, monitor, prevent and respond to threats in a cyber realm 24/7.  "Security operations teams are charged with monitoring and protecting many assets, such as intellectual property, personnel data, business systems, and brand integrity. As the implementation component of an organisation's overall cyber security framework, security operations teams act as the central point of collaboration in coordinated efforts to monitor, assess, and defend against cyberattacks".

### Preparation and Prevention
* Stay informed on current threats (Twitter, Feedly).
* Security roadmap to protect organisation and be ready for worst-case scenario.
* Gather intelligence data on threats, actors and TTPs. 
* Maintenance
* Patching
* Safe and block-listing.

### Monortoring and Investigation
* SIEM tols to monitor activities and look for IoCs.
* Priority to alerts.
* Tier 1 (Junior Level) -> triage

### Response
* SOC team coordinates and takes action against an incident (compromised hosts, network isolation, terminating the malicious act).
